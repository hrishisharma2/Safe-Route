How it works

Each road has a travel time in minutes and a safety score from 1 to 10. A long stretch of unsafe road counts as riskier than a short one:

risk = time × (10 − safety)

Your preference w mixes the two (1 = fastest, 0 = safest), and Dijkstra's algorithm finds the cheapest route:

cost = w · time + (1 − w) · risk

Admins can update road safety scores, so the routes reflect current conditions.
